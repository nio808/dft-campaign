# Reasoning log ({{PROJECT}})

Append-only, project-wide. Every decision that changes what runs, what is accepted or what the paper says, with its ID,
written by `logbook.py decide` and mirrored from the folder REASONING.md. Required fields: Question, Options, Evidence,
Reviews, Choice, Why, Cites. Superseding a decision means a new entry citing the old ID; never edit old entries.
